# Eagle 1.0.2 (61)

Eagle is an iPhone personalization utility for compatible iOS versions that applies visual changes to the Dynamic Island, Dock, Home Screen, wallpapers, passcode keys, and supported Wallet cards. It is intended for experienced sideloading users who understand that private SpringBoard and kernel-level operations can respring or reboot an unsupported device.

Eagle 1.0.2 refines the visual identity and Aura Studio preview, adds a complete independent system-Island workflow, and corrects inherited OTA attribution while preserving the proven Prepare, Dock, and Dynamic Island Aura behavior.

## What changed

- Unified the Eagle mark and wordmark with a restrained spectrum reflection that respects Reduce Motion.
- Refined Home, Access, navigation and Aura surfaces with calmer adaptive contrast in light and dark mode.
- Corrected Aura Studio previews so Glow remains steady, Pulse breathes independently and Rainbow alone rotates the spectrum.
- Paused the Aura preview timeline when no animated mode is selected.
- Added a verified, independent workflow to hide or restore the system Island without changing Island Aura.
- Added Respring now/later actions after the system-Island setting is saved and verified.
- Replaced Home search with direct Stable, Advanced and Laboratory channel controls, including an accurate warning before moving to a lower channel.
- Kept Island Rainbow available in Stable while Advanced and Laboratory continue to expose their additional feature sets.
- Improved wallpaper result reporting and iOS 26-only catalog detection without changing the underlying installer workflow.
- Added Cyanide/0xjohnny attribution to the OTA source, README and an app-bundled notice.
- Preserved Prepare and device compatibility, Dock and Dynamic Island application paths, and the verified iPhone 16 Pro (`iPhone17,1`) profile.

## Compatibility

- Limited testing: iOS 16.7.2.
- Supported Prepare range: iOS 17.0–18.7.1 and iOS 26.0–26.0.1.
- Releases outside those ranges, unverified iOS 16 builds, and current MIE devices remain blocked for safety.
- Primary physical reference: iPhone 16 Pro (`iPhone17,1`) on iOS 18.6.2 (`22G100`).
- New Aura geometry profile: iPhone 14 Pro (`iPhone15,2`) and iPhone 14 Pro Max (`iPhone15,3`).
- The IPA is unsigned and must be signed with the user's normal installation method.

## Español

Eagle es una utilidad de personalización para iPhone y versiones compatibles de iOS que permite modificar visualmente la Dynamic Island, el Dock, la pantalla de inicio, fondos, teclas del código y tarjetas de Wallet compatibles. Está dirigida a usuarios con experiencia instalando IPA que comprendan que las operaciones privadas de SpringBoard y kernel pueden provocar un respring o reinicio en dispositivos no compatibles.

Eagle 1.0.2 refina la identidad visual y la preview de Aura Studio. Glow permanece fijo, Pulse respira de forma independiente y solo Rainbow mueve el espectro. La Island del sistema ahora puede ocultarse o restaurarse sin cambiar su perfil Aura, con verificación y opción de hacer Respring ahora o después. Inicio incorpora controles directos para Stable, Advanced y Laboratory con aviso antes de bajar de canal. Los fondos informan correctamente si iOS no refresca la galería e identifican los elementos exclusivos de iOS 26. También se añadió la atribución correspondiente a Cyanide y 0xjohnny. Preparar y la compatibilidad de dispositivos, las rutas de aplicación del Dock y Dynamic Island, y el perfil verificado del iPhone 16 Pro permanecen intactos.
