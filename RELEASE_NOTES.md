# Eagle 1.0.1 (60)

Eagle 1.0.1 combines the latest visual identity refinements with targeted compatibility fixes while preserving the proven Prepare, Dock, and Dynamic Island behavior on the primary iPhone 16 Pro reference device.

## What changed

- Restored Prepare availability for iPhone 16 (`iPhone17,3`) on iOS 18.5 through the established stable route.
- Added model-specific Aura geometry for iPhone 14 Pro and iPhone 14 Pro Max to improve Dynamic Island and Dock alignment.
- Kept the verified iPhone 16 Pro (`iPhone17,1`) Aura geometry and stable application paths unchanged.
- Preserved Lara's original system Dynamic Island suppression mechanism and added write verification in Aura Studio.
- Replaced the previous decorative brand mark with the unified Eagle mark and refined the Home and Access presentation.
- Improved Aura Studio contrast in light mode without changing its effect engine.
- Icon Studio remains marked as coming soon and Aura Tint remains temporarily unavailable.

## Compatibility

- Limited testing: iOS 16.7.2.
- Supported Prepare range: iOS 17.0–18.7.1 and iOS 26.0–26.0.1.
- Releases outside those ranges, unverified iOS 16 builds, and current MIE devices remain blocked for safety.
- Primary physical reference: iPhone 16 Pro (`iPhone17,1`) on iOS 18.6.2 (`22G100`).
- New Aura geometry profile: iPhone 14 Pro (`iPhone15,2`) and iPhone 14 Pro Max (`iPhone15,3`).
- The IPA is unsigned and must be signed with the user's normal installation method.
