# Eagle 0.3.0 Beta 9

Beta 9 introduces Home Icon Neon and gives Aura Studio the most prominent place on Eagle's Home screen. The proven Dock and Dynamic Island engines remain unchanged.

## What changed

- Added **Home Icon Neon** for the visible app icons on the current Home page.
- Added six quick neon presets, any custom color, and Subtle, Vivid, or Maximum intensity.
- Kept the effect page-scoped: Dock, folders, App Library, Dynamic Island, and Prepare stay outside its transaction.
- Added independent, tag-based removal so Eagle deletes only its own icon-glow views.
- Moved Aura Studio above Styles and Wallpapers, with a **NEW** badge that disappears after the first visit.
- Replaced only the blue Prepare spinner and progress fill with moving rainbow color; Prepare logic and button behavior are unchanged.
- Removed the unverified App Library Neon and Battery X experiments from this release.
- Bumped the app to build `50`.

## Compatibility

- Home Icon Neon is initially allowlisted for iPhone 16 Pro (`iPhone17,1`) on iOS 18.6.2 (`22G100`).
- It is available on the Beta feature channel and can be applied page by page, with up to 24 visible app icons per page.
- Dynamic Island and Dock Aura retain their existing compatibility and safety rules.
- The IPA is unsigned and must be signed with the user's normal installation method.
